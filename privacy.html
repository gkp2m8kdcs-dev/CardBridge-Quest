Privacy Policy

CardBridge Quest

Last updated: October 12, 2026

CardBridge Quest ("we", "our", "the app") is a single-player puzzle game built with your privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Summary

CardBridge Quest does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The game is designed to be fully usable offline. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no tracking.

1. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect or process:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • In-app purchase receipts beyond what Apple's App Store processes

2. Data stored locally on your device

To save your progress across launches, the app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Level completion state, move counts, and best scores
  • Daily challenge history and streak counter
  • Achievements unlocked
  • Settings preferences (theme, sound, haptics, follow system theme)
  • Custom bridges you create with the Bridge Editor

This data never leaves your device. You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Reset All Data.

3. Photos and camera access

CardBridge Quest can optionally use your camera or photo library to create custom card art from your pictures. These features are entirely opt-in.

  • Photos you pick are stored locally inside the app's sandbox.
  • We never upload your photos to any server.
  • You can delete any imported photo from the Gallery view at any time.
  • The app requests camera or photo library permission only when you first use the feature. The exact wording shown by iOS is configured in the app's Info.plist.

4. In-app purchases

Optional paid features, if any, are processed exclusively by Apple via the App Store. CardBridge Quest does not see or store your payment information. Refunds are handled by Apple per their policies.

5. Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, social, or tracking SDKs. The only system-level framework the game relies on beyond SwiftUI is Apple's standard UIKit / AVFoundation for media playback (optional).

6. Children's privacy

CardBridge Quest is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children. Because we collect nothing at all, this policy applies equally to all players.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted in the next app release notes.

8. Your rights

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to export, correct, or delete on our side. On your side, uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset All Data removes all locally stored game data.
