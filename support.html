How to play

  • Swap two cards: tap one card, then tap an adjacent card (up, down, left, or right).
  • Win: make the on-screen grid match the target pattern exactly.
  • Test Bridge: once your pattern matches, tap the button to send the chibi car across.
  • Undo: rewind your last swap if you change your mind.
  • Hint: asks the game to softly highlight one wrong cell.
  • Restart: start the level over from scratch.

Frequently asked questions

The Test Bridge button is greyed out — what now?

The bridge only tests when the grid exactly matches the target pattern. Double-check every cell, especially the corners. If you're stuck, tap Hint to highlight a wrong cell, or Restart for a fresh slate.

My daily streak disappeared. Did I lose it?

Streaks reset only if a full calendar day passes without solving the daily bridge. Tap the daily bridge tile on the Home screen each day before midnight to keep it alive. Changing the device date/time won't help — we read the system's real clock.

Can I make my own puzzles?

Yes. Open the Editor from the Home screen. Paint a 4×4 target pattern, choose a theme, set a move budget, and save. Your creations appear in My Bridges and can be tested, played, or edited again at any time.

How do I add my own photo to the gallery?

From the Gallery tab, tap +. You can take a new picture with the camera or pick one from your library. Photos stay on this device inside the app — they are not uploaded anywhere. You can delete them any time from the same screen.

I want to wipe all my data. How?

Open Settings → Reset All Data. This clears levels, achievements, streaks, custom bridges, and gallery photos. The action cannot be undone, so be sure first.

Does CardBridge Quest collect any data?

No. The game is fully offline. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Everything stays on your device. See the full Privacy Policy.

I lost sound or haptics. Where do I turn them back on?

Open Settings. Toggle Sound Effects and Haptics on. Also check that your iPhone is not in Silent mode (the switch on the left side) — silent mode silences in-app sound effects.

The app crashed. What should I do?

First, make sure your iPhone is running iOS 16 or newer (Settings → General → About → iOS Version). Update if needed, then relaunch the app. If the crash keeps happening, please email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it crashed.

How do I restore my previous purchases?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. Sign in with the Apple ID you used for the original purchase, and any previously bought themes will unlock again on this device.

Troubleshooting checklist

  • Confirm iOS 16 or later is installed.
  • Restart the app (swipe up from the app switcher).
  • Reboot your iPhone if the issue persists.
  • Free up storage if the device is nearly full.
  • Still stuck? Send us a note — the developer reads every message.
