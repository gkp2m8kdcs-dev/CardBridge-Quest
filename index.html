How it works

Match the pattern

Each level shows a target color pattern floating over a tiny river. Tap two adjacent cards to swap them.

Solve with care

Respect a tight move budget. Undo mistakes, ask for a gentle hint, or restart whenever you want a fresh slate.

Test your bridge

Tap Test Bridge and a chibi test car rolls across the structure you built, proving it can hold the weight.
